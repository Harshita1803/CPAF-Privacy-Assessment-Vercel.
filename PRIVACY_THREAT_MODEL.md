# Privacy threat model

The protected records in the experiment are artificial rows generated by this program. The adversary is modeled as a data recipient who knows the published quasi-identifiers and may know auxiliary synthetic attributes. K-anonymity addresses uniqueness of declared quasi-identifier groups in this particular release. Distinct l-diversity and categorical-TV diagnostics assess sensitive-category concentration relative to groups/global release.

The shadow MIA trains a target model on a target-train partition and a shadow model on a disjoint shadow-train partition. Shadow members and shadow nonmembers train an attack classifier; target members and disjoint target nonmembers evaluate it. The attack sees confidence, loss and entropy. This is one attack family, not an exhaustive adversary.

Disclosure proxies report duplicate records excluding the synthetic ID, rarity for exact age/region/city/merchant/amount quasi-identifiers, and nearest-neighbor distances in a fixed normalized mixed feature space. Exact continuous amount makes the rare-record rate structurally high; it is a synthetic uniqueness observation, not a real identity linkage.

These proxies do not prove or quantify re-identification of a person. No real payment data, identities, account details, or real app telemetry are used. The adversary does not have provider internal data. Results do not establish actual Paytm, Google Pay, or PhonePe security, privacy, legal compliance, or fairness.

DP-SGD is a custom experimental implementation with an unverified accountant. Per-example gradient clipping bounds each contribution by C; add/remove sum sensitivity is C, while replace-one sensitivity can be 2C. The code uses independent Bernoulli sampling and Gaussian noise on the clipped sum, then divides by q*n. The accountant does not establish a guarantee for the end-to-end pipeline because its math has not been independently validated, normalization depends on supplied row count, and data selection occurs before DP training. Epsilon is marked unverified and no formal guarantee is claimed.
