# CPAF 2.0: Evidence-Aware Comparative Privacy Assessment and Privacy–Utility Optimization

This research prototype has two deliberately separate tracks: (1) a public-evidence register for Paytm, Google Pay and PhonePe, and (2) privacy/utility experiments on entirely synthetic transaction-like data. The synthetic experiments say nothing about any provider's actual practices.

The inherited implementation includes:
1. A structured evidence matrix and reproducible score calculation.
2. Seven privacy dimensions, weighted scoring, evidence-strength adjustment, coverage and confidence.
3. A large **synthetic** transaction dataset (default 50,000 records) for a separate privacy-engineering experiment.
4. Generalisation and k-anonymity-style suppression, plus l-diversity and t-closeness-inspired diagnostic metrics.
5. CSV/JSON outputs and charts for analysis.
6. **Model-training lab (`cpaf_ml.py`)**: ML models trained on raw / generalised / k-anonymous data, a k-sweep, a membership-inference attack, and differential-privacy training (DP-SGD) with an epsilon accountant.

## Research integrity
The synthetic records are generated by this program. They are **not** actual UPI transactions, and results from the synthetic privacy lab must not be presented as measurements of Paytm, Google Pay or PhonePe. The dated working evidence register currently contains 17 AI-assisted documentary scores and 4 unscored rows; these are provisional assessments of public statements, not independent technical audits or legal-compliance findings. A policy that does not disclose a detail is not proof that the app does not implement it. See `docs/EVIDENCE_REVIEW_20261024.md`.

## Scoring layers
**Layer 1 — Dimension score:** assign 1–5 based on an explicit rubric and evidence.
**Layer 2 — Evidence adjustment:** `adjusted = 3 + (raw_score - 3) × evidence_strength`. Evidence strength ranges 0–1. Weak evidence pulls a score toward neutral 3 rather than treating an unsupported claim as certain.
**Layer 3 — Weighted CPI:** weighted mean across available dimension scores.
**Layer 4 — Confidence:** dimension coverage × mean evidence strength. Report CPI and confidence together.
**Layer 5 — Sensitivity analysis (recommended for final report):** recompute CPI under equal weights and alternative defensible weights; explain whether conclusions change. Do not use a single score without its evidence trail.

## Suggested score rubric
- 1: documented evidence of a substantial privacy weakness in this criterion.
- 2: multiple gaps or broad/unclear practice documented.
- 3: mixed evidence, or policy provides only partial detail.
- 4: clear, specific controls documented with relevant user choices.
- 5: detailed, current, verifiable evidence of strong safeguards and user controls.
- Unknown: not enough evidence; do not silently convert to 1 or 5.

## Methods and caveats
The reusable `cpaf` package validates the fixed synthetic schema, supports hierarchy levels, suppresses complete equivalence classes below k, reports distinct-l and categorical total-variation t diagnostics, and offers independent sorted-block numerical microaggregation. Diagnostics apply only to the selected quasi-identifiers and released table. Categorical t uses total variation against the released table's global sensitive-value distribution. None of these transformations is differential privacy.

## Model-training lab (Layer 6)
All on synthetic data with a synthetic label; none of it measures any real app.
1. **Utility** – logistic regression, random forest and gradient boosting on Raw vs Generalised vs Generalised + k-anonymous data, evaluated on one common test set (ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, Brier).
2. **k-sweep** – records retained and model quality as k grows.
3. **Membership-inference attack** – inherited confidence attack plus an optional shadow-model attack with separate target, shadow, calibration and target evaluation partitions (AUC 0.5 = chance on that attack, not proof of privacy).
4. **Differential privacy experiment** – DP-SGD logistic regression in NumPy (Poisson sampling, per-example clipping, Gaussian noise) plus a custom Renyi accountant. Mechanism coins use OS-backed randomness rather than the reproducibility seed; each candidate uses fixed public delta 1e-5.
Caveats: every DP row is `EXPERIMENTAL_UNVERIFIED`; the numeric epsilon is diagnostic only and `formal_guarantee_claimed=False`. Upstream stratified selection, metric releases, sigma comparison, the custom accountant and floating-point Gaussian implementation are not covered by an established end-to-end proof. A trusted-library migration is documented but not installed or verified in this environment. Do not cite the epsilon as a formal guarantee. See `docs/DP_AUDIT.md`, `docs/DP_METHODOLOGY.md`, `docs/DP_THREAT_MODEL.md`, `docs/DP_LIBRARY_MIGRATION_REPORT.md`, and `docs/IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md`.

## Output integrity

cpaf_outputs contains the original artifacts plus new results in separate run folders. Per-run manifests record seed, size, method, parameters, runtime versions, synthetic provenance and output paths. An empty evidence template is not a scored app comparison. A row contributes only when marked `reviewed` and it includes an HTTP(S) source URL, access date, claim, reviewer and score rationale. This metadata gate does not establish that a source claim is true; human judgments still require independent review. Synthetic results never populate provider scores.

## Suggested extensions for a 30-mark submission
- Add a documented source register with access dates and policy versions.
- Have two reviewers score independently; calculate agreement (e.g. weighted Cohen's kappa) and adjudicate disagreements.
- Run sensitivity analysis for weights (equal weights vs. stakeholder weights).
- Add a small, voluntary, consent-based survey only if approved by faculty; do not collect account numbers, UPI IDs, phone numbers or transaction screenshots.
- Repeat the ML lab over several seeds and report mean ± std; add shadow-model membership inference; try an audited DP library (Opacus / TensorFlow Privacy).
