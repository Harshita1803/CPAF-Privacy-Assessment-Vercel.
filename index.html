What this edition covers

  • Reproducible synthetic data generation with configurable sample size and seed.
  • Raw, generalised and k-suppressed representations.
  • Dataset validation and k/l/t-style diagnostic summaries.
  • Evidence-weighted provisional scores, evidence coverage and rejected-row explanations.
  • JSON result previews and CSV download for the reviewer-entered evidence register.

Limitations

  • The app does not retrieve or independently verify payment providers’ policies; reviewers supply evidence and rationale.
  • Scores are provisional documentary assessments, not technical audits or legal-compliance findings.
  • All experiment records are synthetic, not real customers or transactions.
  • Generalisation, suppression, l-diversity and t-closeness-inspired diagnostics are not differential privacy.
  • The original repository’s more computationally expensive model-training, membership-inference, DP-SGD accountant, and parameter-sweep workflows remain in the preserved research codebase and are not exposed as live web controls in this first Vercel edition.

Use the existing Streamlit deployment for the full original dashboard. This Vercel edition is a separate interface and does not replace it.
